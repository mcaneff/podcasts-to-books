In the third episode of Mostly Wise, we explore:


  If Matt and Jeff could survive Navy SEAL selection, training and hazing.

  Whether the Navy actually has trained animals like killer assassin kamikaze dolphins.

  How the guys deal with their own versions of sobriety.

  Why Love on The Spectrum is potentially problematic.

  What it was like for Jocko to be in the Military on 9/11.

  The story of the gun no manufacturer dared to make after testing it.

  And much more...


Guests


  Jocko Willink is a retired United States Navy officer in SEAL Team 3, an author and a podcaster.

  Matt McCusker is a comedian, writer, author and podcaster.

  Jeff Dye is a comedian, actor, television host and podcaster.
